# Pick your integration

DiceBear runs as an HTTP API, as a library in seven languages, as a CLI, as a
browser-based editor, and as a plugin for Figma. All of them render the same
avatar for the same seed, so you can switch later without your avatars changing.

**Can you point an `<img>` tag at a URL?** Then the
[HTTP API](/integrations/http-api/) is the shortest path. It's free, needs no
installation, and works from any language and platform. If you're unsure, start
here.

**Do you want avatars generated on your own infrastructure?** Use a library.
Your user data stays on your systems and there's no external request per avatar.
Pick your language below.

**Do you need image files rather than running code?** The
[CLI](/integrations/cli/) exports batches to SVG, PNG, JPEG, and more. For a
single avatar, say a default profile picture for your app, the
[Editor](https://editor.dicebear.com) is the no-code way.

**Designing before you build?** [DiceBear Studio](/integrations/figma/), our
plugin for Figma, fills the placeholders in your mockups and tells the developer
which seed and URL render the same avatar in the app.
