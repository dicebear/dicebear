# Dogs

Dogs is the dog next to [Cats](/styles/cats/), with the same scenes and shadows.
Nine head families set the breed, floppy ears for a beagle, a scalloped ball of
fur for a poodle, a dark muzzle for a pug, and three body builds go from a
compact sitter to a long-legged Great Dane. Eleven eye sets include big glossy
puppy eyes, seven mouths include a panting tongue, and most dogs wear a collar,
a tag, a bone or a bandana. Seven fur colors run from cream to chocolate. With
the animation on the head tilts and the tail wags. Generate dog SVG profile
pictures for pet apps, forums and games.
